Padel in Hua Hin

Updated 10 November 2026 from the club registry.

Where the Clubs Are

Clubs are reached by taxi, Grab or scooter, and each club page has directions.

Courts

14 courts at 3 clubs. 2 clubs have indoor courts, 1 has covered courts and 0 are outdoor only.

Booking and Prices

3 of the 3 clubs list an online booking link: 1 on The Padel Society, 1 on Matchi and 1 on Playtomic. Hua Hin Padel Center shows live availability on this site. Courts are booked by the hour and each club sets its own price, shown before you pay.

Hours and Getting There

Most clubs open around 7:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM. Fly into Hua Hin (HHQ) or Bangkok's airports.

Questions About Padel in Hua Hin

How Many Padel Clubs Are in Hua Hin?
3 clubs with 14 courts are listed on Padel Thailand, and 2 of them have indoor courts.
Can I Book a Padel Court Online in Hua Hin?
3 of 3 clubs list an online booking link: The Padel Society (1), Matchi (1), Playtomic (1). The rest take bookings by phone, message or walk-in.
How Do I Get to Padel Courts in Hua Hin?
There is no rail here. Clubs are reached by taxi, Grab or scooter, and each club page has directions.
Which Padel Clubs in Hua Hin Have Indoor Courts?
Padel Of Thailand and Padel Sozial. 1 club has covered courts with a roof and open sides.
When Are Padel Courts in Hua Hin Open?
Most clubs open around 7:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM. Each club page lists the week's hours and shows whether it is open now.
