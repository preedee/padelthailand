Where the Clubs Are

Nonthaburi (2), Khlong Toei (2), On Nut (2), Phrom Phong (2), Phra Khanong (1), Nana (1), Phetchaburi Road (1), Asok · Sukhumvit (1), Chong Nonsi (1), Saphan Taksin (1), Huai Khwang (1), Sam Yan (1), Thonburi Side (1), Bang Na (1), Ratchaprarop (1), Ekkamai (1). 13 clubs are within walking distance of a BTS or MRT station; the map shows walk times.

Courts

64 courts at 20 clubs. 7 clubs have indoor courts, 9 have covered courts and 3 are outdoor only. MOMA Padel Club Sathorn and Vita Padel Ekkamai are opening soon.

Booking and Prices

14 of the 20 clubs list an online booking link: 8 on Playtomic, 4 on the Kross app, 1 on The Padel Society and 1 on LINE. The other 6 take bookings by phone, LINE or Instagram message, or at the desk; each club page has the contact. Pad Thai Padel shows live availability on this site. Courts are booked by the hour and each club sets its own price, shown before you pay.

Hours and Getting There

Most clubs open around 7:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM. Decathlon Bang Yai and Padel Tennis Bangkok (Banthat Thong) are open 24 hours. Fly into Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK).

Questions About Padel in Bangkok

How Many Padel Clubs Are in Bangkok?
20 clubs with 64 courts are listed on Padel Thailand, and 7 of them have indoor courts.
Can I Book a Padel Court Online in Bangkok?
14 of 20 clubs list an online booking link: Playtomic (8), Kross App (4), The Padel Society (1), LINE (1). The rest take bookings by phone, message or walk-in.
Which Padel Clubs in Bangkok Are Near the BTS or MRT?
Baan Padel (BTS Sukhumvit, about 4 min on foot); Bangkok Padel (BTS Sukhumvit, about 4 min on foot); Kross Padel Asoke (BTS Sukhumvit, about 4 min on foot); Kross Padel On Nut (BTS Sukhumvit, about 5 min on foot); Kross Padel Sky (BTS Sukhumvit, about 12 min on foot); MOMA Padel Club Sathorn (BTS Silom, about 12 min on foot); No Drama Padel (BTS Silom, about 9 min on foot); Padel Tennis Bangkok (Banthat Thong) (MRT Blue, about 13 min on foot); Sterling Sport & Wellness (BTS Sukhumvit, about 9 min on foot); Sunshine Padel Club (BTS Sukhumvit, about 12 min on foot); the padel co. (BTS Sukhumvit, about 9 min on foot); Top Padel Bangkok (Airport Rail Link, about 8 min on foot); Vita Padel Ekkamai (BTS Sukhumvit, about 7 min on foot). Walk times are straight-line estimates.
Which Padel Clubs in Bangkok Have Indoor Courts?
Baan Padel, Kross Padel Indoor, No Drama Padel, Pista Samakom, Sterling Sport & Wellness, the padel co. and Vita Padel Ekkamai. 9 clubs have covered courts with a roof and open sides.
When Are Padel Courts in Bangkok Open?
Most clubs open around 7:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM. Decathlon Bang Yai and Padel Tennis Bangkok (Banthat Thong) are open 24 hours. Each club page lists the week's hours and shows whether it is open now.
