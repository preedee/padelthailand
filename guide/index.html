Padel in Thailand

Padel in Thailand: 66 clubs and 220 courts by region, indoor and covered courts, opening hours, how to book, what it costs and how to get there.

Updated 13 November 2026 from the club registry.

View the Network Map

Where the Courts Are

66 clubs and 220 courts in 8 regions. Bangkok has the most, then Samui & Phangan and Phuket.

  • Bangkok: 20 clubs, 64 courts, 7 with indoor courts
  • Samui & Phangan: 18 clubs, 49 courts
  • Phuket: 13 clubs, 55 courts, 5 with indoor courts
  • Pattaya & Bangsaen: 5 clubs, 16 courts, 2 with indoor courts
  • Chiang Mai: 4 clubs, 16 courts, 2 with indoor courts
  • Hua Hin: 3 clubs, 14 courts, 2 with indoor courts
  • Krabi & Lanta: 2 clubs, 5 courts
  • Koh Chang: 1 club, 1 court

Indoor, Covered or Outdoor

18 clubs have indoor courts, 33 have covered courts with a roof and open sides, and 11 are outdoor only. In the rainy months, roughly May to October, a covered or indoor court is the safer booking. The Indoor and Covered filters on the map show them.

Hours

Most clubs open around 8:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM. Padel Tennis Bangkok (Banthat Thong), Decathlon Bang Yai, SIGMA Padel Club and Padel Club @ Samui Karting are open 24 hours.

How to Book

49 of the 66 clubs list an online booking link: 31 on Playtomic, 4 on the Kross app, 3 on the club's own website, 3 on The Padel Society, 3 on Matchi, 2 on LINE, 2 on Facebook and 1 on WhatsApp. The other 17 take bookings by phone, LINE or Instagram message, or at the desk; each club page has the contact.

Hua Hin Padel Center, Pad Thai Padel and WAREHAUS.club show live court availability on this site, so you can see free slots before you book.

Courts are booked by the hour and each club sets its own price, shown before you pay.

Getting There

In Bangkok, 13 of 20 clubs are within walking distance of a BTS or MRT station, and the map shows walk times. Everywhere else, clubs are reached by taxi, Grab or scooter; each club page has directions. Fly into Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK) for Bangkok, Phuket (HKT) for Phuket and Samui (USM), then the ferry to Phangan or Tao.

New and Opening Soon

Questions About Padel in Thailand

How Many Padel Courts Are in Thailand?
66 clubs with 220 courts are listed on Padel Thailand across 8 regions, from Chiang Mai to Phuket. 18 clubs have indoor courts and 33 have covered courts. MOMA Padel Club Sathorn and Vita Padel Ekkamai are listed as opening soon.
Where Can I Play Padel in Bangkok?
20 clubs in Bangkok and Nonthaburi, 13 of them within walking distance of a BTS or MRT station. The Bangkok page shows every club on the rail map with walk times.
Can I Book a Padel Court Online in Thailand?
49 of the 66 clubs list an online booking link: 31 on Playtomic, 4 on the Kross app, 3 on the club's own website, 3 on The Padel Society, 3 on Matchi, 2 on LINE, 2 on Facebook and 1 on WhatsApp. The other 17 take bookings by phone, LINE or Instagram message, or at the desk; each club page has the contact. Hua Hin Padel Center, Pad Thai Padel and WAREHAUS.club show live court availability on this site.
How Much Does Padel Cost in Thailand?
Courts are booked by the hour and each club sets its own price, shown on its booking platform before you pay. Prices vary with the time of day, the court type and the location, so compare on the club pages.
Which Padel Clubs in Thailand Are Open 24 Hours?
Padel Tennis Bangkok (Banthat Thong), Decathlon Bang Yai, SIGMA Padel Club and Padel Club @ Samui Karting list 24-hour opening. Most other clubs open around 8:00 AM and close around 10:00 PM.
Is Padel Played Indoors in Thailand?
Yes. 18 of the 66 clubs have fully indoor courts and 33 have covered courts with a roof and open sides, which keep play going through the rainy months from about May to October. Filter the map by Indoor or Covered.
