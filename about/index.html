How This Site Works

Every Club Is Listed the Same Way

The Map, the Clubs Page and the Filters Treat Every Club Equally. Each Booking Button Goes to Where the Club Actually Takes Bookings.

The Padel Society (TPS) Runs the Booking for Its Own Clubs. When Live Booking Arrives on This Site, TPS Clubs Get an In-Page Booking Button and a Live Availability Board. They Get No Extra Ranking, Size or Placement.

Where the Listings Come From

Clubs Come From a Registry That Is Refreshed Weekly From Public Sources, Then Checked by Hand. Tournaments and Events Come From Organisers, Clubs and Our Own Research. Anything Sent Through the Submission Forms Is Reviewed by a Person Before It Goes Live.

Photos and Posters

Photos Are Shown With a Credit Line. If a Photo Is Yours and You Want It Changed or Removed, Tell Us. Posters Are Shared With Permission.

Walk Times and Prices

Walk Times Are Straight-Line Estimates From the Nearest BTS or MRT Station, Not Measured Routes. Prices Are Shown Only When a Club Has Told Us; Otherwise We Say So.

Privacy and Analytics

We Use Google Analytics With Anonymous Settings to See Which Pages and Buttons Help Players: Page Views, Clicks on Booking and Directions Links, and Which Filters Get Used. We Do Not Collect Names or Emails Through Analytics, and Google Signals and Ad Personalisation Are Turned Off.

Analytics Cookies Stay Off Until You Press Accept. Before You Choose, Google May Still Receive Anonymous Page Views Without Cookies. You Can Change Your Choice Any Time With Cookie Settings in the Footer.

Names and Emails Sent Through the Submission Forms Are Used Only to Check the Submission and Are Deleted 12 Months After It Is Reviewed.
